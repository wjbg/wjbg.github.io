I am an Associate Professor in the [Production Technology](https://www.utwente.nl/pt) group at the University of Twente, specializing in (thermoplastic) polymer composites, with a strong focus on manufacturing. I received both my M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering and my Ph.D. on the topic of Fiber Placement of Thermoplastic Composites from the University of Twente. In my work, I try to bridge fundamental material science and industrial application by developing experimental characterization and predictive manufacturing simulation tools.


## Research Vision {#research-vision}

My research is centered around a fundamental principle: **manufacturing determines structure, and structure determines performance.** Consequently, without accurate structure prediction, it is impossible to reliably predict performance. To achieve right-first-time manufacturing of parts with predictable performance, therefore, advanced process simulation tools should include (multi-scale) structure development.

Making progress in this domain requires a systematic approach on two fronts:

-   **Experimental Analysis** | Developing (novel) characterization methods to identify, analyze, and quantify the physical phenomena underlying manufacturing processes.
-   **Numerical Modeling** | Translating these physical insights into robust constitutive models and numerical simulation tools that can be used predict material behavior under industrial manufacturing conditions.

## Teaching {#teaching}

My teaching philosophy centers on developing a deep intuition for engineering principles, where a **solid understanding of fundamental underlying physics** is key to solving complex problems. I prioritize active dialogue and interactive engagement with students through questioning rather than one-way lecturing, supported by hands-on assignments that encourage them to explore concepts themselves while practicing self-motivation and independent steering in their M.Sc. assignments and theses.

My teaching activities include:

-   **Composites** (M.Sc.) | Addresses the mechanical performance of laminated composites, as well as composite materials and manufacturing.
-   **Phase Transformations in Manufacturing** (M.Sc.) | Focuses on structure development and processing-structure-property relationships for advanced materials.
-   **Aircraft Structures** (B.Sc.) | Teaches engineering concepts for lightweight structures in aircraft applications.
-   **Graduation Theses** | I regularly supervise M.Sc. graduation projects and student research assignments.
